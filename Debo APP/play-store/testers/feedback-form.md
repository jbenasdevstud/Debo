# Tester feedback form

Send around day 7–10 of the closed test, e.g. as a Google Form or simply as a message. Keep the answers: Google's production application asks what feedback you received and what you changed.

Lithuanian first, English below (use the English questions for lv / pl / de testers, or translate).

---

## Lietuviškai

1. Kaip dažnai atsidarei Debo per šias dvi savaites? (kasdien / kelis kartus per savaitę / vos kartą ar du)
2. Ką išbandei? (gliukozė, maistas, insulinas, sportas, grafikai ir statistika, ataskaita gydytojui, priminimai, valdiklis pradiniame ekrane, atsarginė kopija, CGM per Health Connect)
3. Ar kas nors neveikė, užstrigo ar išsijungė? Kas tiksliai ir kada?
4. Kas buvo neaišku ar sunku rasti?
5. Kas patiko labiausiai?
6. Ko trūko? Ką pridėtum?
7. Kokį telefoną naudoji (gamintojas, modelis, Android versija)?
8. Ar sergi diabetu ar padedi juo sergančiam? (nebūtina atsakyti)
9. Ar rekomenduotum Debo pažįstamam, kuriam reikia tokios programėlės? (taip / galbūt / ne) Kodėl?

---

## English

1. How often did you open Debo during these two weeks? (daily / a few times a week / once or twice)
2. What did you try? (glucose, food, insulin, exercise, charts and statistics, doctor report, reminders, home screen widget, backup, CGM via Health Connect)
3. Did anything not work, freeze or crash? What exactly, and when?
4. What was unclear or hard to find?
5. What did you like most?
6. What was missing? What would you add?
7. Which phone do you use (brand, model, Android version)?
8. Do you live with diabetes or help someone who does? (optional)
9. Would you recommend Debo to someone who needs an app like this? (yes / maybe / no) Why?
