## Part 2 – About your app

**Who is the intended audience of your app?**
Adults (18+) living with type 1 or type 2 diabetes, and people who help them, who want a simple diary for glucose, food and insulin. Debo is available in Lithuanian, English, Latvian, Polish and German.

**Describe how your app provides value to users.**
Debo lets people with diabetes log glucose, meals (with carbohydrates from a built-in food catalogue) and insulin in a few seconds, and see their day on one chart. It shows averages and time in range, creates a PDF report to take to the doctor, reminds them to check glucose and change sensors, and can import CGM readings through Health Connect. All data stays on the phone, there are no ads and no account is needed. Debo is a logbook and general information app – it does not calculate doses or give treatment advice.
