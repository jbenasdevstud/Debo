DEBO – PUBLISHING ON THE APP STORE (iPhone and iPad)
=====================================================

WHAT IS IN THIS PACKAGE
  1-App/Debo.swiftpm.zip          The app itself. You upload it to Apple from Swift Playgrounds on the iPad.
  2-Privacy-policy/debo-privacy.zip  Updated privacy policy (with the iPhone/iPad section). Upload to Cloudflare.
  3-Store-texts/                  Texts to copy into App Store Connect:
                                    listing-lt / en / lv / pl / de  – name, subtitle, description, keywords…
                                    app-store-connect               – answers for every form + review notes
  4-Icon/Debo-icon-1024.png       The App Store icon (already inside the app; keep it for reference).

Apple does NOT take a zip file. You upload the app from Swift Playgrounds (step 4).
Everything else is typed / pasted into the App Store Connect website.


STEP 0 – WAIT FOR APPLE TO APPROVE YOUR DEVELOPER ACCOUNT
  - You need the e-mail "Welcome to the Apple Developer Program".
  - Until then the account says "Enrollment pending" and nothing can be uploaded.


STEP 1 – PUT THE NEW PRIVACY POLICY ONLINE  (Safari, about 3 minutes)
  1. Download 2-Privacy-policy/debo-privacy.zip (leave it zipped).
  2. Open https://dash.cloudflare.com → Workers & Pages → debo-privacy.
  3. Create deployment (or "Upload assets") → choose debo-privacy.zip → Save and deploy.
  4. Open https://debo-privacy.pages.dev → section 5 must be "iPhone and iPad".


STEP 2 – OPEN THE APP IN SWIFT PLAYGROUNDS  (iPad)
  1. In Files: tap 1-App/Debo.swiftpm.zip to unzip it. You get ONE item "Debo.swiftpm" (that is normal).
  2. Press and hold Debo.swiftpm → Move → iCloud Drive → Playgrounds (or On My iPad → Playgrounds).
  3. Delete any older Debo first (also from Files → Recently Deleted).
  4. Open Swift Playgrounds → Locations → Playgrounds → tap Debo.
  5. Check: no red errors. If there are, send a screenshot to Claude.


STEP 3 – CREATE THE APP IN APP STORE CONNECT  (Safari: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com)
  1. Accept any agreements shown at the top.
  2. Apps → "+" → New App:
       Platform:         iOS
       Name:             Debo – diabeto palydovas   (or the English name from listing-en)
       Primary language: Lithuanian
       Bundle ID:        com.debo.app
       SKU:              debo-ios
       User access:      Full Access
  3. If com.debo.app is not in the list:
       https://developer.apple.com/account → Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles → Identifiers → "+"
       → App IDs → App → Bundle ID (Explicit): com.debo.app → Register. Then repeat step 2.
       If Apple says it is taken: use lt.debo.app and tell Claude (the app must be changed too).


STEP 4 – UPLOAD THE APP  (Swift Playgrounds on the iPad)
  1. Open Debo → tap the app settings (the app icon / name at the top → "App Settings").
  2. Team: sign in with your developer Apple ID and choose your team.
  3. Bundle Identifier: com.debo.app (the same as in step 3).
  4. Tap "Upload to App Store Connect" and follow the prompts.
  5. Wait 10–30 minutes for the e-mail "The following build has completed processing".


STEP 5 – TEST WITH TESTFLIGHT  (private, only you)
  1. App Store Connect → Debo → TestFlight.
  2. Encryption question → "None of the algorithms mentioned above".
  3. Internal Testing → "+" → group "Me" → add yourself.
  4. iPhone and iPad: install the TestFlight app → sign in → Install Debo.
  5. Test: typing (name, glucose, food search), drop-downs, time fields, reminders (a notification
     arrives?), Print/PDF report, Backup export + restore, dark mode.
  6. Anything wrong → screenshot or screen recording to Claude, then upload a new build (step 4 again).


STEP 6 – FILL IN THE STORE PAGE  (App Store Connect → Debo)
  Use 3-Store-texts/app-store-connect.txt for every answer. In short:
  - App Information: category Medical (secondary Health & Fitness); Regulated medical device: No;
    EU trader status: Not a trader (if you earn nothing from the app); age rating questionnaire.
  - Pricing and Availability: Free, all countries (or at least the EU).
  - App Privacy: "No, we do not collect data from this app"  →  shows "Data Not Collected".
  - Privacy Policy URL: https://debo-privacy.pages.dev
  - Support URL: https://debo-privacy.pages.dev
  - Version page (iOS App 1.0), for each language (add languages with the language menu at the top):
      name, subtitle (App Information), promotional text, description, keywords, "What's New"
      → copy from listing-lt / en / lv / pl / de.
  - Screenshots: ready in 5-Screenshots/ – iPhone 6.9" only (version 1.0 is iPhone only):
      drag iPhone-6.9-01 … 06 into App Previews and Screenshots → iPhone 6.9".
      Optional header: header-3840x1646.png → Header and Search Results → Header → Upload.
  - App Review Information: sign-in NOT required; your name, phone, e-mail;
      Notes → paste the review notes from app-store-connect.txt.
  - Build: choose the build you tested in TestFlight.


STEP 7 – SUBMIT
  1. Check the list at the end of app-store-connect.txt.
  2. "Add for Review" → "Submit for Review".
  3. Apple usually answers in 1–3 days.
  4. If rejected: send Apple's message to Claude – most problems can be fixed and resubmitted.


IMPORTANT
  - Every new upload needs a higher build number. Swift Playgrounds usually raises it; if it complains,
    tell Claude to raise bundleVersion in Package.swift.
  - Debo must stay a logbook: no dose calculations, no advice based on a reading, no alarms/predictions.
