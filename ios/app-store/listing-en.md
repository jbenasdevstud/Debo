## Description (max 4000)
Debo is a calm, simple diary for people living with diabetes. Log your glucose, food and insulin in seconds and see your day at a glance.

QUICK DAILY LOGGING
• Glucose readings with time, optional ketones and a short note
• Insulin doses for pens or pumps, with your own rapid-acting and basal insulin
• Meals by breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks, with carbohydrates, protein and fat
• Favourite meals you can log again with one tap
• Undo for anything you delete by mistake

FOOD CATALOGUE
Over 250 foods – everyday products, Baltic dishes like cepelinai and šaltibarščiai, soups, sauces, drinks and fast carbs for lows – with carbohydrates per 100 g and common portions, plus a glycemic index where one is published. Add your own foods in seconds, or scan a product’s barcode to fill in its nutrition values.

CHARTS AND REPORTS
• Daily glucose chart with your insulin doses
• Averages, time in range and a daily profile over weeks and months
• A PDF report for your doctor, created on your iPhone – print it or save it as a PDF
• Searchable history by day, food name or note

REMINDERS
Notifications for glucose checks (with a night pause), a check 2 hours after meals, long-acting insulin, a weekly summary and your own daily reminders.

DEVICES AND SUPPLIES
Track when you last changed your sensor, pen or infusion set and get a reminder when your chosen interval ends. Count your sensors, test strips and needles, and get a reminder before you run out or something expires.

GENERAL GUIDES
Always-available step-by-step general information for low and high glucose, a 15-minute timer you start yourself, and an exercise guide with typical glucose responses for 40 activities.

ALSO INCLUDED
• Today’s steps and the last 7 days from your iPhone’s motion sensor
• mmol/L ↔ mg/dL converter
• Backup to Files and restore on a new iPhone or iPad
• Export all entries as a spreadsheet (CSV)
• Light and dark mode
• Lithuanian, English, Latvian, Polish and German

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
Your health entries are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. No account, no ads, no data selling, no tracking.

IMPORTANT
Debo is a logbook and general information app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not calculate insulin doses, interpret your readings or give treatment recommendations. Always make treatment decisions together with your doctor or diabetes care team.
